# Decision template (OpenSpec changes)

Copiar a `openspec/changes/{change-name}/decisions.md` al capturar decisiones del change.

```markdown
# Decisions: {change-name}

**Change**: {change-name}
**Status**: Active
**Created**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Decisions

| ID | Domain | Decision | Rationale | Status |
|----|--------|----------|-----------|--------|
| DEC-001 | ARCH|TECH|UX|PROC|SEC | … | … | Proposed|Accepted|Superseded |
```

Promover decisiones Accepted cross-cutting a `docs/decisions/{domain}/` y ADRs cuando aplique.
